Safe arrival for baby Amara in Douala

"When I found out I was expecting, my biggest fear wasn't the pain, but the cost. JoyFund connected me to a community that didn't just give money, they gave me peace of mind." — Nadine S.

Nadine’s story is one of many. In the heart of Douala, access to safe delivery services can be the difference between a celebration and a tragedy. This JoyFund is dedicated to ensuring 50 more mothers like Nadine receive the vital medical care they need during labor and delivery.
